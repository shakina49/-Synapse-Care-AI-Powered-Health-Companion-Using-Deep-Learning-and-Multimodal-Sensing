Capture tips

Small tweaks to improve detection accuracy.

Full body in frame
Make sure head, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles are visible.
Good lighting
Avoid heavy shadows. Face the light source if possible.
Camera angle
Side view works best for squats, push-ups, planks, and deadlifts.
Single person
Only the person performing the exercise should be in the frame.
